How it works

Three steps. Zero padding.

  1. 1

    Tell us what you want

    You already find homes online yourself — most buyers do. Tell us your neighborhoods, budget, and must-haves, and we set up your search and alerts.

  2. 2

    We run the math

    For every home you like, we run a comparative market analysis and model the seller's net proceeds — what they actually keep after commissions — so your offer is grounded in numbers, not guesswork.

  3. 3

    You offer with leverage

    Because our commission is a fraction of the traditional rate, your offer can be lower on paper and net the seller more. That's leverage no traditional agent can match.

Forced transparency

No hype. Proof, instead.

  • Escalation clauses tied to net offers

    Our escalation clauses are written against the highest bona fide net offer — what the seller actually keeps — not the highest headline number.

  • Proof of competing bids

    We demand to see the competing bids we're told to beat. You will never be pushed into an inflated price on someone's word alone.

  • Data over salesmanship

    Every offer is grounded in a comparative market analysis and the seller's net-proceeds math — reviewed with our attorney before it goes out.
